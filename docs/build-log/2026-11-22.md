# 2026-11-22: Physics, then a gesture studio

- Moved the ceiling from 100 g to 250 g. The real parts (1505 motors, 3S 650 mAh battery) come to
  about 165 g, which is fine: the model says thrust-to-weight of 3.3 to 5.6 and about 4 minutes of hover.
- Built the altitude model and tested a 0 to 1 m step. With a laser height sensor it settles inside
  +-2 cm in about 1.5 s. A barometer alone cannot do it, so the laser sensor goes on the list.
- Built the roll and pitch model. Biggest lesson: if the battery sits 20 mm off-centre the drone
  starts at 48 degrees of tilt, and at 40 mm it is out of control. New requirement R10: centre of
  gravity within 5 mm, and the battery gets an enclosure.
- Ported both models to MATLAB and checked they give the same graphs.
- Built Gesture Studio: a web page that records and names hand and body gestures.
- First gesture set was too similar (finger counts 3, 4 and 5 were confused with each other).
  Redid it with clearly different shapes, and the new set had zero wrong-gesture triggers.
- Added a minimum-confidence rule (new requirement R11) and a command table with two-byte frames.
  Kill gets a lower confidence bar (40%) because it is far from everything else and missing it is worse.
- Recorded 10 seconds of body idle. It never came close to kill or hover. Hand idle still to do.
- Checked the CAA rules: from 100 g a Flyer ID is needed, and an Operator ID is optional without a camera.
  The old note saying a sub-100 g drone needs neither is true, but WASP is now well over 100 g.
- Next: record hand idle, build the Gesture Link (hand and body together, talking to a simulated
  drone), check out before Black Friday ends, then the frame in Fusion 360.
